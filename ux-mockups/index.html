UI/UX propositions

Simplifier sans enlever l'information.

The current app is functional but often feels like accounting software: many menus, many tables, and long forms. These prototypes keep the same information while changing the order of attention: first the daily decision, then the details, then the advanced tools.

Design principle 1 Put daily actions first: sell, buy, collect production, add payment, record expense.
Design principle 2 Show "what needs attention" before charts and full tables.
Design principle 3 Hide expert controls behind "More filters", "Reports", and "Advanced details".
Option A

Daily command center

Best for the home/dashboard and non-technical daily operators.

  • One-click daily actions
  • Simple alerts and next steps
  • Compact business health summary
Open mockup Option B

Simple registers

Best for list pages: transactions, caisse, clients, fournisseurs, charges, LC.

  • Readable rows instead of dense tables
  • Quick tabs for common filters
  • Exports and advanced filters stay secondary
Open mockup Option C

Guided entry flow

Best for creating transactions, production entries, expenses, and payments.

  • Step-by-step forms
  • Plain language labels
  • Always-visible summary before saving
Open mockup Production

Production stock board

Best for daily production by building and stock tracking for non-technical users.

  • Building-first stock cards
  • Produced - sold - lost formula
  • Quick daily entry and missing-entry alerts

Recommended final product direction

I would combine all three: Option A as the default landing page, Option B as the reusable pattern for every list/register page, and Option C as the standard pattern for add/edit flows. That gives non-technical people a calm daily workflow while preserving the full accounting depth for managers.
